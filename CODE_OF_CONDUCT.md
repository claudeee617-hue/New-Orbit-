# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the ORBIT project a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, appearance, background, experience, identity or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact.
- Accept feedback and focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Share evidence, acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's credentials, source code or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention without consent, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed. For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub. For security issues, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This project adapts the Contributor Covenant principles. It is not a legal contract.
